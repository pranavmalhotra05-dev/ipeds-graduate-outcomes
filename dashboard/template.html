Graduate Outcomes Dashboard: master's and doctoral completions, IPEDS 2020-2023

Four views of one data source. Small cells are hidden, not zero. Ratios are not completion rates. Definitions and limits.

Role views are filtered views of one source, not access control. Every tab reads the same suppressed data (program counts under 10 are withheld). Switching tabs does not unlock any additional data. This is a static HTML dashboard; a separate Tableau Public workbook uses the same data.

Graduate dean: institution versus peer group

The completions-to-enrollment ratio divides one year's master's and doctoral completions by fall graduate enrollment. It is a ratio, not a completion rate, and has no cohort meaning. Enrollment exists only at institution level, so the ratio is always for all levels combined.

Provost: national and sector overview

National totals, how much the suppression threshold hides, and an external benchmark. The NSF Survey of Earned Doctorates (SED) medians are a separate field-level source; they are not institution cohort data and cannot be matched to IPEDS completions.

Definitions and limits

  • Ratio, not a cohort rate. "Completions-to-enrollment ratio" = master's and doctoral completions in an academic year divided by fall graduate enrollment of the same IPEDS file year (a prior-fall variant is also shown). The students counted in each are not the same people, enrollment includes certificate students and part-timers, and program lengths differ. Do not read it as a graduation or completion rate.
  • IPEDS Graduate Rates has no graduate cohorts. The IPEDS Graduation Rates (GR) survey covers undergraduate cohorts only, so no graduate cohort completion rate can be computed from these sources.
  • No causal claims. Differences between institutions, fields, peer groups or years are descriptive. They do not show that any program, policy or institution caused an outcome.
  • 2023 enrollment is provisional. Fall 2023 enrollment is a provisional release, so 2023 ratios may change. 2024 completions are provisional and are not used. Completions are final/revised files for 2020-2023.
  • Suppression rules. Program cells (institution x 6-digit CIP x award level x year) with fewer than 10 completions, including zero, are hidden and shown as "hidden". Complementary suppression also hides the next-smallest cell in an institution x level x year x CIP family group, and then in the institution x level x year group, when exactly one cell would otherwise be hidden, so a hidden cell cannot be recovered by subtraction. A hidden cell can therefore be larger than 9 after complementary suppression. Institution, field and national totals under 10 are hidden. Suppressed cells cannot be told apart from missing programs except by the suppression flag. The rule is simple, not an optimal secondary-suppression algorithm.
  • Unit of count. Counts are awards (degrees), not students; first majors only (second majors excluded); CIP 2020; award levels 7 (master's), 17, 18, 19 (doctoral). Year 2023 means academic year 2022-23.
  • Small-n warnings. A warning is shown when a percentage rests on fewer than 5 program cells, on fewer than 30 completions, or when a peer group has fewer than 10 institutions. Percentages across institutions mix new and closed programs with growth.
  • SED benchmark. NSF Survey of Earned Doctorates, Table 1-12 (median years to research doctorate, by trend broad field), cycles nsf26326 (latest year 2025) and nsf25349 (latest year 2024). It is a separate, field-level, national source covering research doctorates only; it has no institution cohorts, and the extracted table has no respondent counts, so no n can be shown for it. The CIP-to-SED field mapping used elsewhere is an approximate analyst mapping, not an official crosswalk.
